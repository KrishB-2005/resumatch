Title: Staff Software Engineer, Data Platform
Company: Meridian Health Systems

Position Overview
Meridian operates the clinical data platform used by 200 hospitals. This role
sits on the ingestion team and reports to the Director of Data Engineering.

Minimum Qualifications
1. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
2. 8+ years of professional software engineering experience
3. Proficiency in Java or Scala
4. Experience with Apache Spark and distributed data processing
5. Working knowledge of Kafka for event streaming

Preferred Qualifications
1. Experience with Snowflake or another cloud data warehouse
2. Familiarity with HIPAA and healthcare data standards
3. Airflow or a comparable orchestration framework
4. Contributions to open source data tooling

Responsibilities
1. Design and operate ingestion pipelines processing 40TB per day
2. Partner with clinical informatics to model new data domains
3. Participate in a weekly on-call rotation
