Full Stack Engineer (Founding)

Our mission
We think hiring is broken and we are building the tools to fix it. Two founders,
both engineers, backed by a seed round from people we respect.

The role
You would be our third engineer. We are looking for someone with at least four
years of experience who is comfortable owning a feature from database schema
through to the React components a user touches. You should be fluent in
TypeScript and have real production experience with Node.js. We run everything
on AWS and would like you to be comfortable there, though you can learn it here
if you are not.

We care much more about whether you can ship than which frameworks you have
used. That said, familiarity with Next.js and Tailwind CSS would let you move
faster on day one, and anyone who has worked with Postgres will feel at home.
